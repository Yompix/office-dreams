YURK VPN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Developer: Yompix · Contact: Telegram @yurkvpn_bot

What YURK does

YURK VPN routes your device's internet traffic through an encrypted tunnel (VLESS over TLS / Reality) to our servers in the Netherlands, the USA and Singapore, using Android's VpnService. The app's core function is the VPN.

What we do NOT collect

We do not log which websites or apps you use, your DNS queries, the content of your traffic, or your real IP address next to your activity. Our servers run with access logs turned off.

What we store

  • Telegram account id and name — you sign in with Telegram; we need it to link your subscription.
  • Subscription data — plan, expiry date, payments (Telegram Stars / CryptoBot invoice ids and amounts), referral balance.
  • Traffic amount per day (bytes only, no destinations) — to apply the monthly traffic limit of your plan.

We don't sell or share this data with anyone. Payments are processed by Telegram or CryptoBot under their own policies.

Permissions

VPN (VpnService) — to create the encrypted tunnel; Android asks you before the first connection. Notifications — the "Protected" notification with a Disconnect button. Internet — to connect.

Deleting your data

Write to @yurkvpn_bot with the word "delete" — we remove your account and subscription data within 7 days.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

YURK шифрует трафик устройства и направляет его через наши серверы. Мы не храним, какие сайты и приложения вы открываете, DNS-запросы и содержимое трафика. Храним только Telegram id и имя, данные подписки и платежей, объём трафика по дням (только байты) — для лимита тарифа. Данные никому не продаём и не передаём. Удаление — напишите «удалить» в @yurkvpn_bot.
